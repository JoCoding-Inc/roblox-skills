# Windows built from the kit: what the judges named, build by build

The record behind the rules at "What the judges named, as rules" in `roblox-ui-mobile` section 4: windows of six screen types and moods built from that section by smaller models, each judged blind (two judges a sitting, sums out of 100), 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-22. Each table names what the judges marked down, the rule taken from it and the kit call that does it now. Quotes are the judges' own words. Section 4 holds the rules as they stand now.

**A shop built from this text, and what it was marked down for** (2026-11-21, blind, two judges, sums out of 100). A smaller model given a brief for another screen and another mood (a supply shop for a horror game) wrote its own skin (`Kit.look` matte and tight, `Kit.tone("heavy")`, a typewriter display face) and its own structure from `Layouts.shop` (a rail with counts, a timed offer, a grid of 16 items, a detail pane with stat bars and a state button, a stamp on purchase) in 25 minutes: 78, against 75 for a designer's window, 85 for the missions window of the sitting before, and 61 for `Layouts.shop` itself as it then was. What the judges named is now in the kit or in this table:

| Named by the judges | Rule | In the kit |
|---|---|---|
| On a category change the old and the new cards lay over each other at angles; the heading had changed while the old items still showed | Cards of a grid never overlap: the old ones fade in 0.1 s, the new ones are dealt in from 0.1 s, 30 ms apart; the heading changes with the cards | `Kit.grid` (`grid:show(keys)`); `Kit.refresh({ heading })` |
| A selection outline stood in an empty grid before its card arrived | A selection mark arrives after its card | `grid:select(key)`: the ring leaves during a `show()` and comes back when the cells are in place |
| "PAYING" held 0.75 s before anything happened; three small coins | The result of a press is on screen within 0.5 s. No waiting state without motion: the flight starts on the press (5 pieces, 0.3 s, 40 ms apart), the item bursts and its button changes as the first piece lands, the counter runs down in 0.35 s | `Layouts.shop` `buy` |
| A HUD button still showed past the window's corner while the window opened | Every HUD item is out of sight before the window lands (0.2 s) | `Kit.dock`: an item fades out in the first 0.1 s of its slide, and each side leaves in its own order |
| The example had only two counters: no way into the shop, a HUD that did not react | A window opens over a HUD that has its button, and that HUD gives way and comes back | The three layouts now build the menu buttons and the action button and dock them |
| A purchase burst stayed in its grid slot and played over the next category's item | One card per item, keyed; an effect belongs to its card and leaves with it | `Kit.grid` |
| Nothing said "no" when the price was too high | A press that cannot go through answers at once: the button shakes, the counter turns to the `warn` colour for the same time | `Kit.shake`, `short()` in the layouts |
| HUD icons with no plate read as default UI on a dark world | HUD buttons sit on plates with the counters' corner and outline, in every skin | `Kit.button` with `role = "menu"` |
| The wrong layout on the first frame (from the builder's report) | Choose the phone or the large layout from `Kit.screen()`: a client's first frames report a placeholder viewport | `Kit.screen()` |
| A card stayed lifted after a tap on a touch screen (from the builder's report) | Hover is for a mouse only | `Kit.press` |

**An inventory built from this text by another model at medium effort** (2026-11-21, blind, two judges, sums out of 100; the brief: a sea chest for a pirate adventure game with filters, rarities, three equipment slots, a detail card with stats, equip and sell). It wrote its own skin (weathered wood, parchment, brass; a blackletter display face), took the rail and grid of `Layouts.shop`, added a worn pane and a detail column, and used `Kit.grid`, `Kit.fly` with the item's own icon (to the slot, and the old item back to its card), `Kit.count`, `Kit.shake` and `Kit.burst` by rarity. 23 minutes, lint clean on four devices. Scores: this build 82, the designer's window 79, the horror shop above 73, the kit's own shop example 63. Named as its best part by both: the equip (the item flies to its slot, the old one flies home, the total counts up, the button changes). What it was marked down for:

| Named defect | Rule | In the kit |
|---|---|---|
| The window was a see-through shell for 3 frames with the HUD showing through it (both judges) | A window is opaque by its second frame; the scale carries the entrance | `Kit.open` (changed after this sitting) |
| The whole detail card blinked to 30% on an equip (both) | A change dips the texts and images that changed, not the surface they sit on | `Kit.refresh` (now 60%) |
| A filter with 5 items left 55-70% of the grid as one flat dark field | Free cells show empty sockets, or the cells grow to fill two rows | `Kit.grid` `sockets` |
| The scrolled grid jumped to its top in one frame before the filter fade | Fade first, move the view unseen, then deal | `Kit.grid` |
| The sell button showed a coin and a price next to EQUIP: it read as a buy price | A button that takes or gives currency carries its verb: "SELL 1,200" | - |
| Rarity names and a caption at 10 px on brown; an empty band over the buttons when an item has two stats | 12 px bold is the floor for a caption; a pane with a variable count of rows centres them or moves the buttons up | - |
| A grid row cut by the frame with no fade | A scrolling grid that cuts a row fades it at the edge (`Kit.list` does for rows) | - |

**A season pass built from this text** (2026-11-21, blind, two judges, sums out of 100; the brief: a season pass for a fast team shooter with a level panel, a two-lane reward track of 30 levels, a detail strip, missions, claim and claim all). No skeleton in the table fits it; the smaller model built it from the kit's calls with its own sci-fi skin in 29 minutes, lint clean on four devices. Scores: this build 76, the designer's window 84, the inventory above 77, the horror shop 71. Named as its best part: the staged open, the HUD that leaves and returns in steps, the claim-all chain. What it was marked down for:

| Named defect | Rule | In the kit |
|---|---|---|
| "Many labels are 8-9 px", "the title reads Flore Pock" (both judges). Every label was TextSize 14 or more: the skin's two faces draw the smallest letters of the 48 measured (capitals 0.45 of TextSize) | Size text by the letters, not by TextSize: a face that draws small gets a larger TextSize. A display face whose lowercase is small is set in capitals and digits only; names in mixed case go in the body face | `Kit.textSize(px, face)`, the warning in `Kit.look`; the table in the typography topic |
| Flat cards with no material: one fill and one thin line on every piece (both) | Matte is not flat: a dark panel keeps a top-to-bottom fall of about 10%, a 1 px light line inside its top edge, and the one to three pieces to press carry light of their own | `Kit.look({ topLight = true, lift = 0.1 })`, `Kit.glow` |
| The "+800" landed on the window's top border (both; named in two sittings before) | A "+N" never lands on a window: it takes the free side of its counter | `Kit.count` (changed after this sitting) |
| A see-through window in the first frame of the open; 0.2 s between the press on close and the window moving (both) | The window is opaque from its first frame and starts to leave with the press | `Kit.open`, `Kit.close` (changed after this sitting) |
| Slivers of the next cards between the lane labels and the first card | A track that scrolls under a fixed column is clipped at that column's edge and fades there | - |
| A big number with its "/ 30" 120 px away | A value and its unit or total sit on one baseline, 8-12 px apart | - |
| Two banners (level up, claim all) as plain bars across the track for about 1 s each | A banner over working content is under a third of its height and holds 0.5-0.6 s | `Kit.stamp` |

**A forge built from this text** (2026-11-21, blind, two judges, sums out of 100; the brief: a forge for a storybook fantasy RPG with craft and upgrade tabs, recipes, materials, a quantity stepper and an upgrade roll). Another model at medium effort copied the kit, wrote its own warm skin (iron, leather, parchment; a calligraphic display face over a book serif) and built the screen in 26 minutes, lint clean on four devices in six states. Scores: this build 68, the designer's window 82, the season pass above 80, the inventory 81. Named as its best parts: the craft sequence (the item glows while it forges, bursts, flies to the bag and the count ticks), a needle that sweeps the chance bar on an upgrade, the NOW and AFTER columns, the close that brings the HUD back in order. What it was marked down for:

| Named defect | Rule | In the kit |
|---|---|---|
| The open: the HUD left, 0.5 s of empty screen, then the HUD came back with the window and left again (both). The build's own tour destroyed the whole GUI after each loop, built it again and opened the window from code at once, as the kit's demo tour then did | A tour or demo that loops never destroys and rebuilds the GUI: close the window, put the data back, let the HUD rest for a moment, then press the HUD button that opens the window. The open starts on the frame of the press | `window_kit_demo.luau` (`api.reset`, the Missions button opens the window; changed after this sitting) |
| A tab change showed a mixed frame: the old rows still there, the new tab's footer over the last of them, the detail pane an empty frame (both) | The rows that leave are gone before anything moves over them; a pane whose whole page changes (craft and upgrade) swaps pages: the old page fades out, the new one fades in as its parts arrive | `list:show` (the footer fades with the rows that leave; changed after this sitting), `Kit.swap` (new) |
| The recipe column about 45% empty under three rows; a row of material slots with its third slot empty (both) | A list well is never more than about a quarter empty: size it to its rows, or fill the rest with the next locked or coming rows (dimmed, with what opens them). A row of slots has as many slots as the item needs, centred | - |
| Material counts "14/3" read as ambiguous (both) | A cost in materials shows the amount needed first and large, the amount owned small beside it ("3" over "have 14"), with a check when met and the `warn` colour when short | - |
| The result shown as the button's label ("IRON GAUNTLETS!"), held about 1.0 s with nothing moving before the flight (both) | A result inside a window holds 0.35-0.5 s and keeps moving while it holds (burst, glow, a small turn), then the item flies; the longer tier holds of the effects topic are for full-screen reveals. The button turns to its done state; it never carries the result | - |
| "-150" and "-10" under the HUD counters read as flat grey and unstroked (both). The build passed a salmon colour, which sat in the kit's fixed dark green outline | A spend is red with an outline in a dark shade of its own colour, as large as a gain | `Kit.count` (changed after this sitting: a spend is red by default, the outline follows the text's colour) |

**A talent tree built from this text** (2026-11-22, blind, two judges, sums out of 100; the brief: a talent tree for a bright Viking-island survival game with three branches, ranks, a capstone and a detail pane). It scored 78 (39 + 39) against 85 for the reference in that sitting. Named by both judges unless marked:

| Named defect | Rule | In the kit |
|---|---|---|
| "'Talents' reads as 'Calents' at small size" (one): a blackletter face at 32 px for the title and 22 px for the talent names | A decorative face (blackletter, script, brush) is for one large word at most; names and labels a player reads at a glance go in a rounded or plain display face | - |
| The banner of the biggest moment: "a flat blue bar with plain yellow text, left-aligned over the GATHERING row", "covers the branch nodes" | The moment's banner is centred on the whole window, or sits just under the node it names; it wears the window's own material with a stroked name (the judges asked for 28-32 px), and what it covers darkens | `Kit.stamp` warns when it lands more than a tenth of the window's width off its middle, or over a button, with nothing dimmed (`options.dim`) |
| The selection: "a thin white circle with four handle dots, like an editor gizmo, not a game selection" (one) | A selection is a soft glow in the accent colour (`Kit.glow`), the node a little larger (the judge asked for 1.08) and a short ease when it moves (`Kit.slideTo`); no handle dots or thin white outlines | - |
| A "Learn this first" tip covers a branch label | A tip sits beside or above what it points at, with a pointer, and never over another label | - |
| A "-2 points" floater "tiny, on the window's top frame line" | A spend rises from the counter it changes, inside the window, as large as a gain and stroked (`Kit.count`) | - |
| About 90 px of empty parchment between the text and the requirement line of the detail pane | A pane has no empty block: its lines follow one another with the button under them, or the space holds an image or a line of lore | - |
| Pale dots on the four outer corners of the window "read as stray marks" | Decoration sits inside the frame in its material (rivets in the wood), never on the corners outside it | - |
| "The tree is unreadable until about 0.8 s: every node waits for its stagger" (one) | A set of fifteen or more small items arrives row by row or with 15-20 ms between items (the judge's ask), so the whole set reads by about 0.6 s | - |

**A collection log built from this text** (2026-11-22, blind, two judges, sums out of 100; the brief: a Sea Log for a calm undersea exploration game with habitat tabs, a creature grid with silhouettes for the creatures not found yet, a detail card, a habitat progress bar with milestones, and a new find as the big moment). It scored 71 (34 + 37) against 77 for the reference and 83 and 80 for two earlier builds in that sitting. Named as its best parts: one ocean palette with rarity colours, the silhouette that fills with colour, the stars that fly to the counter with a count-up, the HUD that leaves and comes back in steps. Named by both judges unless marked:

| Named defect | Rule | In the kit |
|---|---|---|
| The open: "row 2 is fully visible at 0.27s while row 1 cascades in afterwards" (one). The build staged the first row of its grid in `Kit.open`; the other rows came with the window | Every card of a set arrives in its turn, in reading order, row 1 first, 30 ms apart (the judges' ask) | `Kit.open` (changed after this sitting: same-size cards beside a staged group join it, and a group of small pieces is dealt one by one) |
| "About 1.1 s of nothing happening between selecting the silhouette and the reveal" (one), "About 1.2 s of dead hold with nothing moving before the new-find reveal starts" (other). The build waited 0.45 s, then breathed a glow and shook the dark card by 3-4 px | The answer to a press starts on the next frame. The time before a reveal is a build-up the player sees: the card swells and trembles, a light rises round it, sparks fly in (one judge asked for "cell shake, rising glow, sparkles", the other for 0.3 s or less of idle) | `Kit.charge` (new) |
| "Reveal order is off: the name, Epic tag and NEW badge appear while the art is still a silhouette" (one) | The picture first, its words 0.15 s after it, each with a small pop (the judge's ask) | `Kit.refresh` `delay` (new) |
| The banner of the find: "a small flat purple bar with a tiny label, and the dim turns the whole window muddy grey" (one), "a plain purple bar with an about 8 px 'EPIC FIND!' label, and it covers the octopus cell the stars burst from" (other) | The banner of a moment is a reward piece: a ribbon in the tier's colour with tails, a stroked title of 20 px or more (the judges asked for "20 px or more" and twice the size), the found thing's picture on a medal, rays for the higher tiers. What darkens is a band behind the title in the window's own hue, never the whole window, and the title stays off the card the moment came from | `Kit.banner` (new), `Kit.stamp` `dim` (now a band) and `avoid` (new) |
| "The NOT FOUND YET toast covers the progress bar and its milestone chips" (one; the other named it too) | A short answer to a press sits just above what was pressed, with a tip pointing at it, inside the window and over nothing else (both judges asked for it above the selected cell) | `Kit.callout` (new) |
| "The new-find badge '1' overlaps '9/12' on the selected Coral Reef tab" (one) | A count on a tab clears every text on it: after the tab's name on its line, or on the tab's top-right corner 6 px outward (the judge's ask) when the header leaves room above the tab | `Kit.badge` `beside` (new) |
| Names "cut off at the top of the scrolled grid, with no fade mask" (one), "half-cut names at the top edge with no fade mask" (other) | A scrolling well fades at both edges while there is more past them (both judges asked for 16 px) | `Kit.list` and `Kit.grid` `fade` (both edges; changed after this sitting) |
| "The frame and header are plain dark panels and look closer to default kit UI" (one), "Window chrome is flat: plain rounded rectangles, a plain title, little depth or material for a 'wondrous' mood" (other) | Material in the game's own theme: a trim along the header ("a themed header trim (wave or coral strip)", "a trimmed header plate"), a quiet motif on the window body ("a subtle bubble or caustic texture"), an inner shadow at the top of a well, a stroked display title | `Kit.trim`, `Kit.card` `inset` and `pattern` (new) |

After this sitting the new calls and options were checked on stills in a lab window (the banner and its band, the title moved off the card, the callout, the trim, the pattern, the fades at both edges, the badge after a tab's name, the charge) and the open order by numbers; they have not been judged yet.
